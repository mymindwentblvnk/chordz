The three things to know

  1. The scale. Major: W-W-H-W-W-W-H. Natural minor: W-H-W-W-H-W-W.
  2. The chord types. Major: I ii iii IV V vi vii°. Minor: i ii° III iv v VI VII. (Uppercase = major, lowercase = minor, ° = diminished.)
  3. Skip - pick - skip - pick. From the chord's scale note, skip one note, pick the next, skip one, pick the next.
